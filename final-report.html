Rook Dispatch · Final report · 27 Oct 2026

Release 4.2 cut 30 seconds from every call, and four responders stopped getting called at all.

It wasn't the season and it wasn't the new proximity weights. The ping timeout went from 90 to 60 seconds. More pings ran out, the code counted each one as a "no," and nothing ever gave the points back.
